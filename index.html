How This Works

Two Ways To Do This.
We Chose Ownership.

The common model

Rent out the lot

A charging company installs and owns the hardware on your land. You collect a rent check. The company keeps the charging revenue.

Our model

Own the hardware

Through the Supercharger for Business program, EVII Mission Hills buys and owns the V4 Power Cabinets and posts outright. They are listed on the Tesla network and appear in Tesla navigation. Every session bills to us.

  1. 01
    The raise buys the dirt. The site is held today by TYLD LLC and is contracted to transfer into EVII Mission Hills, Inc., the company you would own shares in. $805,000 of this raise funds that purchase. No ground lease. No sale-leaseback. The operating company will own the land it operates on, which is also what allows the C-PACE construction financing to close.
  2. 02
    We own the equipment. V4 Power Cabinets with the site controller built in, plus the V4 posts, purchased by EVII Mission Hills, Inc. Not leased. Not hosted.
  3. 03
    Drivers find us automatically. The site is listed on the Tesla network and routed to in Tesla navigation. No customer acquisition spend to fill the stalls.
  4. 04
    The revenue is ours. Charging fees, LCFS credits, food, coffee and vending all bill to the company you own shares in.

We picked a side. It won.

This is a NACS site. Every major automaker selling new cars in the US has moved to that connector. And because these stalls sit on the Tesla network, a driver who needs a charge is routed here by the navigation already in the dash. As driver-assistance keeps advancing, that routing gets more automatic, not less.

Power

4,000A of headroom

LADWP has committed 4,000 amps to this address. The build uses V4 Power Cabinets with the site controller integrated, the current generation, not the one being phased out.

The Lot

Every square inch, used

On a 9,150 sq ft site we fit 18 fast chargers, two canopies, a commissary, a robotic barista, vending, a washroom, entertainment screens, audio zones, security and lighting, short and long term bike parking, a staff building, and a pad for a 20-foot food truck. Nothing wasted, nothing filler.

Uptime

99.95% is the standard we plug into

Tesla reported 99.95% average Supercharger site uptime for 2024 in its Impact Report. That is the network these stalls join. On our side, this is not our first site. EV Initiative has operated its own charging network as a Charge Port Operator since 2018, on its own management software. Operators, not landlords. That is what Experience-Driven Charging™ takes.

And the demand curve is not ours to worry about. California has approved a plan to end the sale of gasoline-only vehicles. One in four cars sold in the state is already electric. The cars are coming whether or not the infrastructure is ready. Mission Hills is ready.
